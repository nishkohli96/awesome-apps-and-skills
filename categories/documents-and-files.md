# 📄 Documents & Files

_Create, edit, store and share documents, slides, PDFs and files._

## Documents & Slides

- **[Codex Slides](https://github.com/nexu-io/codex-slides)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="app" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/app-1f883d?style=flat-square"><br>
  Open-source AI slide studio inside Codex that researches, designs and exports decks as PPTX or PDF. Needs no extra API keys.<br>
  🏷 `slides` `presentations` `pptx` `ai-agents`
- **[Documenso](https://documenso.com/)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="web-tool" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/web--tool-4f46e5?style=flat-square"><br>
  Open-source DocuSign alternative for sending, signing and managing documents with templates and workflows. Use the hosted app or self-host it.<br>
  🏷 `e-signature` `documents` `self-hosted` `open-source`<br>
  💰 freemium
- **[Presenton](https://presenton.ai/)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="web-tool" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/web--tool-4f46e5?style=flat-square"><br>
  AI presentation maker that turns prompts and documents into editable PPTX or PDF decks. Use the cloud app, self-host the open-source version or automate it through the API.<br>
  🏷 `slides` `presentations` `pptx` `open-source` `api`<br>
  💰 freemium
- **[WordCraft](https://github.com/storytold/wordcraft)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="app" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/app-1f883d?style=flat-square"><br>
  Open-source word processor written in Rust, a clean-room take on Microsoft Word.<br>
  🏷 `word-processor` `documents` `rust`

## Files & PDF

- **[delphitools](https://delphi.tools/)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="web-tool" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/web--tool-4f46e5?style=flat-square"><br>
  Collection of small utilities for images, PDFs, audio, video, colour, text and dev work. No logins or data collection, everything runs locally in your browser.<br>
  🏷 `utilities` `pdf` `image-tools` `privacy` `open-source`<br>
  💰 free
- **[Docling](https://github.com/docling-project/docling)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="library" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/library-0a7ea4?style=flat-square"><br>
  Open-source toolkit that parses PDFs, Office files and other documents, with layout and table understanding, into Markdown, HTML or JSON for generative AI. Runs locally.<br>
  🏷 `document-parsing` `pdf` `markdown` `rag` `open-source`
- **[Ente Photos](https://ente.com/)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="app" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/app-1f883d?style=flat-square"><br>
  Open-source, end-to-end encrypted photo storage and sharing app for phone and desktop, with on-device face detection. You can self-host it, and 10GB is free.<br>
  🏷 `photos` `encrypted-storage` `privacy` `self-hosted` `open-source`<br>
  💰 freemium
- **[iLovePDF PDF to Markdown](https://www.ilovepdf.com/pdf-to-markdown)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="web-tool" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/web--tool-4f46e5?style=flat-square"><br>
  Online converter that turns a PDF into a Markdown file, keeping headings, tables and lists.<br>
  🏷 `pdf` `markdown` `file-conversion` `online-tool`<br>
  💰 freemium
- **[LocalSend](https://localsend.org/)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="app" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/app-1f883d?style=flat-square"><br>
  Free open-source app that shares files between nearby devices over your local network, with no internet needed.<br>
  🏷 `file-sharing` `airdrop-alternative` `cross-platform` `offline`
- **[MarkItDown](https://github.com/microsoft/markitdown)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="library" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/library-0a7ea4?style=flat-square"><br>
  Python tool that converts PDFs, Office documents and other files to Markdown for use with LLMs.<br>
  🏷 `markdown` `file-conversion` `llm` `python`
- **[Nextcloud](https://nextcloud.com/)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="web-tool" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/web--tool-4f46e5?style=flat-square"><br>
  Open-source platform for file storage, sharing and collaboration that you can self-host or get from a provider.<br>
  🏷 `file-sync` `collaboration` `self-hosted` `open-source`<br>
  💰 freemium
- **[PDF Tools](https://github.com/sammyZi/pdf-tools)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="app" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/app-1f883d?style=flat-square"><br>
  Free Android app with 24 PDF tools and a document scanner. Everything runs on-device, with no ads or sign-up.<br>
  🏷 `pdf` `android` `document-scanner` `offline` `open-source`
- **[pdfme](https://github.com/pdfme/pdfme)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="library" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/library-0a7ea4?style=flat-square"><br>
  Open-source TypeScript library for generating PDFs from templates, with a WYSIWYG template designer and a PDF viewer. Works in the browser and Node.js.<br>
  🏷 `pdf` `pdf-generation` `template-designer` `typescript`
- **[Stirling PDF](https://www.stirling.com/)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="web-tool" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/web--tool-4f46e5?style=flat-square"><br>
  Open-source PDF editor you can run on your own infrastructure, with a free plan and paid team plans.<br>
  🏷 `pdf` `editor` `self-hosted` `open-source`<br>
  💰 freemium
- **[Syncthing](https://syncthing.net/)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="app" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/app-1f883d?style=flat-square"><br>
  Open-source continuous file synchronization that keeps files in step between two or more computers in real time, with no third-party server holding your data.<br>
  🏷 `file-sync` `peer-to-peer` `privacy` `open-source`
- **[Unstract](https://github.com/Zipstack/unstract)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="app" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/app-1f883d?style=flat-square"><br>
  Open-source platform that uses LLMs to turn PDFs, images and scans into structured JSON from natural-language prompts. Deploy it as an API or ETL pipeline, with any LLM provider.<br>
