# 📈 Career & Productivity

_Resume, job search and personal productivity resources._

- **[ai-job-search](https://github.com/MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search)** ![starter](https://img.shields.io/badge/starter-fb8500?style=flat-square) ![python](https://img.shields.io/badge/python-3776ab?style=flat-square)<br>
  Claude Code framework you fork to evaluate job postings, tailor CVs, write cover letters and prep interviews locally.<br>
  🏷 `job-search` `cv` `cover-letter` `claude-code`
- **[Cal.com](https://cal.com/)** ![web-tool](https://img.shields.io/badge/web--tool-4f46e5?style=flat-square)<br>
  Open-source scheduling tool with booking links, calendar sync, reminders and video calls. Can be self-hosted.<br>
  🏷 `scheduling` `calendar` `booking` `open-source`<br>
  💰 freemium
- **[career-ops](https://github.com/career-ops-hq/career-ops)** ![skill](https://img.shields.io/badge/skill-d97757?style=flat-square) ![node](https://img.shields.io/badge/node-339933?style=flat-square)<br>
  Open-source AI job search agent that scores jobs against your CV and tailors resumes, cover letters and interview prep.<br>
  🏷 `job-search` `resume` `ats` `claude-skills`
- **[freehire](https://freehire.me/)** ![web-tool](https://img.shields.io/badge/web--tool-4f46e5?style=flat-square)<br>
  Open-source job search engine indexing tech jobs from company career boards, with a CLI, MCP server and Chrome extension.<br>
  🏷 `jobs` `job-search` `cli` `mcp`<br>
  💰 free
- **[Granola](https://www.granola.ai/)** ![app](https://img.shields.io/badge/app-1f883d?style=flat-square)<br>
  AI notepad for meetings that writes notes and actions without a meeting bot. Available on desktop and mobile.<br>
  🏷 `meeting-notes` `ai-notes` `transcription`<br>
  💰 freemium
