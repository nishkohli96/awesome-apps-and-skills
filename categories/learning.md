# 🎓 Learning

## Books & Courses

- **[AI Engineering from Scratch](https://github.com/rohitg00/ai-engineering-from-scratch)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="guide" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/guide-9a6700?style=flat-square"><br>
  Free, open-source course of 542 lessons across 20 phases, where you build model internals, retrieval pipelines and agent runtimes in Python, TypeScript, Rust and Julia.<br>
  🏷 `ai-engineering` `course` `llm` `ai-agents` `learning`<br>
  💰 free<br>
  🔗 [website](https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com)
- **[Hands-On Large Language Models](https://github.com/HandsOnLLM/Hands-On-Large-Language-Models)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="guide" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/guide-9a6700?style=flat-square"><br>
  Official code for the O'Reilly book, with notebooks for every example on how LLMs work and how to use them.<br>
  🏷 `llm` `book` `notebooks` `learning`
- **[Hugging Face AI Agents Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/agents-course/unit0/introduction)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="guide" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/guide-9a6700?style=flat-square"><br>
  Free course that takes you from beginner to building AI agents, with hands-on units, assignments and a certificate.<br>
  🏷 `ai-agents` `course` `huggingface` `learning`<br>
  💰 free

## Learning Tools

- **[DeepTutor](https://github.com/HKUDS/DeepTutor)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="app" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/app-1f883d?style=flat-square"><br>
  Open-source personalized AI tutor that learns from your documents, with practice questions, a learning journal and a CLI.<br>
  🏷 `ai-tutor` `education` `personalized-learning` `self-hosted`
- **[OpenMAIC](https://github.com/THU-MAIC/OpenMAIC)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="app" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/app-1f883d?style=flat-square"><br>
  Open-source multi-agent interactive classroom that turns a topic into a guided, interactive AI learning session.<br>
  🏷 `ai-classroom` `education` `multi-agent` `self-hosted`<br>
  🔗 [demo](https://open.maic.chat/)

- **[StudyArena](https://studyarena.com)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="web-tool" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/web--tool-4f46e5?style=flat-square"><br>
  Compare three anonymous AI answers to a study question, vote, then reveal the models. Free account required.<br>
  🏷 `education` `ai` `learning`<br>
  💰 freemium
