  🏷 `ai-engineering` `course` `llm` `ai-agents` `learning`<br>
  💰 free<br>
  🔗 [website](https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com)

  🏷 `courses` `certificates` `online-learning` `upskilling`<br>

## Learning Tools

- **[DeepTutor](https://github.com/HKUDS/DeepTutor)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="app" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/app-1f883d?style=flat-square"><br>
  Open-source personalized AI tutor that learns from your documents, with practice questions, a learning journal and a CLI.<br>
  🏷 `ai-tutor` `education` `personalized-learning` `self-hosted`
- **[Learn Anything](https://learn-anything.xyz/)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="website" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/website-218bff?style=flat-square"><br>
  Free platform that connects you to the best resources for learning any topic.<br>
  🏷 `learning` `resources` `discovery`<br>
  💰 free
- **[OpenMAIC](https://github.com/THU-MAIC/OpenMAIC)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="app" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/app-1f883d?style=flat-square"><br>
  Open-source multi-agent interactive classroom that turns a topic into a guided, interactive AI learning session.<br>
  🏷 `ai-classroom` `education` `multi-agent` `self-hosted`<br>
  🔗 [demo](https://open.maic.chat/)
- **[StudyArena](https://studyarena.com)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="web-tool" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/web--tool-4f46e5?style=flat-square"><br>
  Compare three anonymous AI answers to a study question, vote, then reveal the models. Free account required.<br>
  🏷 `education` `ai` `learning`<br>
  💰 freemium
- **[Transcribe Video: To The Text](https://video2text.ttt.so)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="app" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/app-1f883d?style=flat-square"><br>
  iPhone app that transcribes videos, YouTube/Vimeo links and live voice into searchable text, with speaker split, summaries and TXT/DOCX export.<br>
  🏷 `transcription` `speech-to-text` `ios` `notes`<br>
  💰 freemium<br>
  🔗 [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/transcribe-video-to-the-text/id6757491608)
- **[Turbo AI](https://www.turbo.ai/)** <img align="absmiddle" alt="web-tool" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/web--tool-4f46e5?style=flat-square"><br>
  AI study assistant that turns files, videos and lectures into notes, flashcards, quizzes and lessons.<br>
  🏷 `study` `flashcards` `ai-notes` `education`<br>
  💰 freemium
